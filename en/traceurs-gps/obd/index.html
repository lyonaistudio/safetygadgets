SIM card

Which SIM card should I use?

The tracker sends its position over the 4G network, so it needs a SIM card with a little data (not included). No need for a big plan.

  1. 1A 4G plan with dataAny carrier works. A tracker uses very little data: a small plan is plenty.
  2. 2Disable the PIN codeFirst put the SIM in a phone and turn off the PIN code in the settings: the tracker can't enter it.
  3. 3Insert and plug inSlide the SIM into the tracker, plug it into the OBD port and follow your vehicle from the app.

In France, for example, Free's €2/month plan (unlimited calls and texts, 1 GB of data, no commitment) works perfectly.
